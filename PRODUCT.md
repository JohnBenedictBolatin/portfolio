# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Employers and recruiters reviewing John Benedict Bolatin's work, experience, and professional learning.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents John's software and technology projects, experience, skills, and credentials so employers can assess his work and background.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines project evidence across web, mobile, and connected systems with the learning behind the work.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a public Next.js portfolio on desktop and mobile. They can follow project links, review experience and technologies, and verify credentials at their issuing platforms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public routes include the home page, projects, experience, stack, and certifications.
- Credential records are authored as local portfolio content; the site has no live credential API.
- Credential dates and earned items must match supplied documents or the owner's public issuer profiles. Do not infer missing dates or imply Salesforce professional certification from Trailhead superbadges.
- The owner's Appkademiya certificate confirms NetOps Certified Engineer (NTO-101), issued August 22, 2026; verification is available at https://www.appkademiya.online/verify.

## Brand Commitments

- The owner is John Benedict Bolatin, a fourth-year BS Information Technology student specializing in Mobile and Internet Technology at National University Fairview.
- The portfolio is employer-facing.
- Preserve the existing monochrome portfolio identity, chrome mark, fonts, and black background with restrained ASCII characters.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portfolio credential PDF supplied by the owner, containing LinkedIn Learning certificates and completed Salesforce Trailhead superbadge screenshots.
- Public Credly profile at https://www.credly.com/users/john-benedict-bolatin, including IBM SkillsBuild and Cisco digital badges.
- Public Salesforce Trailhead profile at https://www.salesforce.com/trailblazer/stqarwvywg3zzlktnq.

## Product Principles

- Make real work and verifiable learning easy for an employer to scan.
- Name each credential and its issuing organization accurately.
- Keep evidence close to the credential it verifies.
- Avoid claims or dates that the available evidence cannot support.
