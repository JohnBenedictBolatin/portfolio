## overview

iKonek 176B brings communication and service requests for Barangay 176B into one web-based system for residents and barangay officials.

## community updates

Barangay officials can post updates, and residents can respond through comments. The familiar, social feed helps keep local announcements and conversations in one place.

## assistance and document requests

Residents can request event assistance, such as borrowing chairs or tents, and submit requests for barangay documents, including barangay and business permits.

## faster document processing

Document parsing and autofill reduce repeated data entry. Submitted details can be prepared for printing, helping residents and officials move through the request process more quickly.
